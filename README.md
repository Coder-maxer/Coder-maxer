# Hey there

I'm Ijaz, a data scientist and computer science grad based in Calgary, Alberta. My main stack is Python, TypeScript, Next.js, and PostgreSQL.

I'm the founder and CEO of [CHAD Software Development & Consulting](https://chadsoftwaresolutions.ca), a two-person studio I run with my brother. *And yes, we are really named CHAD. If you know the meme, you know how we do things: we min-max everything we build and give it our best, from the first commit to launch day.*

We come in as a third party contractor, so you work straight with the engineers building your software. No middleman, no middle management.

We learn how your company actually runs and turn that into code. If the data is a mess, we clean it up, show you where you are losing money using your own numbers, and build the in house tools that fix it. We phase out the legacy systems we can, and wrap the ones we cannot in a UI people can stand to use.

Instead of hiring a software engineer, a front end dev, a data person and an IT department, you get two engineers who do all of it and work inside your systems like part of the team.

Most of what we build lives in private client repos, so what is public here is a small slice of it.

**<img src="https://api.iconify.design/tabler:tools.svg?color=%2396825f&amp;height=18" height="18" alt="" />&nbsp; A few things we have worked on:**

- **Wind turbine failure analysis** for the DSMLC x Enbridge case competition at the University of Calgary. Enbridge handed us 30 GB of turbine data, about 50 million rows, and asked what was going wrong out there. We cleaned and organized it, found what was failing, and brought them a solution. The dashboard we presented it on was Next.js and Recharts, statically exported, with no backend to babysit. It was my first case competition and our team won it.
- A strategy case on revitalizing **Cochrane, Alberta** for the **Urban Systems** competition. I helped my brother's team put it together, and they won. That made two competitions inside two months, and we have not lost one yet.
- A **first pass AI reader for commercial roofing bid packages**. Feed it the spec book, the drawings and the addenda, and it returns one standardized summary: scope per roof area, which manufacturers are sole source versus or approved equal, what the addenda changed, and a page citation behind every claim.
